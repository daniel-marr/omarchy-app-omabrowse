# OmaBrowse

OmaBrowse is an Omarchy-native build of [Helium](https://github.com/imputnet/helium-linux), a Chromium-based browser. It follows the Omarchy theme and runs side by side with stock Helium.

## Privacy

- OmaBrowse does not send crash reports to Helium's servers. Crash uploads are disabled in this build.
- OmaBrowse does not check for updates against Helium's servers on Linux.
- Your profile, history, cookies and passwords are stored only on your computer, in `~/.config/net.imput.omabrowse`.
- Features that use third-party services (for example, Google services or search suggestions) follow the settings you choose in the browser.
